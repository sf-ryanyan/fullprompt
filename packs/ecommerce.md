# Scene pack — E-commerce product 电商产品

## Pack questions

- 有产品实拍图吗？ A. 有（推荐，图生图/图生视频最准） ｜ B. 没有，先出概念图
- 用途？ A. 主图白底 ｜ B. 场景图/种草 ｜ C. 短视频展示
- 风格？ A. 干净极简 ｜ B. 生活场景 ｜ C. 强对比/潮流

## Pack checks

- **Product accuracy:** a real product must keep its real shape, color, label and size. Prefer editing from the user's product photo with a preservation line: "preserve the product's shape, color and label exactly".
- **No false claims:** no fake effects, fake before/after, invented certifications or reviews.
- **Labels and logos:** do not let the model redraw a real label; keep the real one static from the photo, or use "a plain unlabeled bottle" for concept images.
- **Hands:** product held by hands → medium shot, simple grip, hands partly out of frame.

## Look Card (default)

`Look: clean soft studio light, neutral background, true-to-life color, 50–85mm lens feel, crisp product edges.`

## Templates

### 1. Hero shot 主图
```
{Product with material, color, finish} centered on {white seamless background / stone slab}.
Composition: {front three-quarter view}, product fills 60% of the frame, soft natural shadow underneath.
Camera: 85mm lens feel, deep focus on the product.
Light: large soft key light from the left, gentle rim light separating the product from the background.
Style: clean commercial product photography, true-to-life color.
No text, no extra props.
```

### 2. Lifestyle scene 场景种草
```
{Product} on {real setting: bathroom shelf / desk / picnic blanket}, {time of day}.
Props: {2–3 props that fit the use}, slightly out of focus.
Composition: product on the right third, eye level, empty space on the left.
Light: {soft window light}.
Style: realistic lifestyle photography, warm natural palette, a small natural imperfection ({a folded towel corner}).
No text; the product label stays exactly as in the reference.
```

### 3. Product video 展示视频 (image-to-video)
```
The reference image is the opening frame; preserve the product's shape, color and label exactly.
0–3s: Soft light slowly sweeps across the product from left to right.
3–6s: {one small motion: water droplets slide down / fabric settles / steam rises}.
Camera: one slow 15-degree arc to the right, product stays centered and sharp.
Light: soft studio key with a moving highlight.
Sound: quiet ambient tone, one soft {click / pour} sound.
Nothing else changes; no text.
```

### 4. Unboxing 开箱
```
Top-down view of a {plain kraft / matte black} box on a {light wood} table.
0–3s: Two hands lift the lid slowly.
3–6s: The hands fold back tissue paper to reveal {product}.
6–8s: The hands move out of frame; the final frame holds on the product.
Camera: locked-off top-down, 35mm lens feel.
Light: soft daylight from a window above.
Sound: paper rustle, gentle music.
No text, no logos on the box.
```

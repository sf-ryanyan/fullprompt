# Scene pack — Real estate 房产

## Pack questions (pick up to 3 for Step 3)

- 用途？ A. 房源展示 ｜ B. 短视频获客 ｜ C. 品牌形象
- 房子风格？ A. 现代简约 ｜ B. 传统/欧式 ｜ C. 我发房源照片（推荐，最准）
- 时间段？ A. 白天明亮 ｜ B. 黄昏暖光 ｜ C. 夜景亮灯

If the user has real listing photos, recommend image-to-video from those photos so the property is shown accurately.

## Pack checks

- **Fair housing:** describe the property and lifestyle, not who should live there. Show a natural mix of people. Avoid phrases like "perfect for young families" or "ideal for couples".
- **Accuracy:** do not invent features the property does not have (pool, view, size). For a real listing, generated scenes must be labeled as illustrations or based on the real photos.
- **Architecture:** slow straight camera moves; "straight vertical lines"; 24–35mm lens feel.
- **No text** on signs, house numbers or "for sale" boards — add in editing.

## Look Card (default)

`Look: bright natural light, warm muted palette, clean and uncluttered rooms, 24–35mm lens feel, straight vertical lines, slow camera.`

## Templates

### 1. Showing 带看
```
Inside {home type}, {time of day}. An agent in {outfit} opens the front door and steps out of frame as {1–2 visitors, mixed ages and backgrounds} walk into {first room with key feature}; at most 2 people stay in focus.
0–3s: The door opens to reveal {feature}.
3–6s: The visitors pause and look up at {ceiling / windows}.
6–9s: They walk toward {view}; the final frame holds for one second.
Camera: gimbal-smooth follow from behind at shoulder height, 35mm lens feel.
Light: {natural window light}.
Style: realistic lifestyle commercial.
Sound: footsteps on {floor material}, soft room ambience, no music.
No text, no logos; walls stay straight.
```

### 2. Exterior / drone 外景航拍
```
{Home: stories, siding, roof, landscaping} on {street / setting} at {golden hour / blue hour with warm interior lights}.
Camera: one slow {drone pull-back / lateral glide / crane up} from {start} to {end}, straight vertical lines, 24–35mm lens feel.
Small motion: {trees sway lightly, porch lights glow}.
Style: premium real estate film, muted palette.
Sound: birds and a light breeze, no music.
No text, no signs, no people.
```

### 3. Interior, no people 室内空镜
```
{Room} with {3 key materials or features}. {Time of day} light through {windows}.
Small motion: {a sheer curtain drifts, sunlight slides across the floor, steam rises from a cup}.
Camera: one slow push-in from {doorway} to {focal point}, eye level, 24mm lens feel, straight lines.
Style: architectural photography look, 40% empty space in the frame.
Sound: quiet room tone, distant birds.
The room stays exactly the same; no furniture moves; no text.
```

### 4. Lifestyle / party 派对生活
```
{Living space} at {early evening}, {view through windows}.
Foreground: {Character Card × 1–2}. Background: {2–4 guests of mixed ages and backgrounds}, softly out of focus, chatting.
0–4s: {host action}.
4–7s: {interaction}.
7–10s: {closing moment}; the final frame holds for one second.
Camera: one slow dolly-in from {entrance} to {window}, eye level, 35mm lens feel, shallow depth of field.
Light: {golden hour + warm pendant lights}.
Style: realistic lifestyle commercial, natural skin texture.
Sound: {soft acoustic music, light laughter}, no dialogue.
No text, no logos; relaxed, not rowdy.
```

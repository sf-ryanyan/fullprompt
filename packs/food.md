# Scene pack — Food & restaurant 美食探店

## Pack questions

- 拍什么？ A. 招牌菜特写 ｜ B. 出餐/制作过程 ｜ C. 店内环境和氛围
- 风格？ A. 诱人暖色 ｜ B. 清新自然 ｜ C. 夜宵烟火气
- 有实拍图吗？ A. 有（推荐图生视频） ｜ B. 没有

## Pack checks

- **Real dishes:** for a real restaurant, base visuals on the real dish photos; do not invent portions or ingredients that are not served.
- **Appetite cues:** steam, sauce shine, crisp edges, cheese pull, sizzling sound — pick 1–2, not all.
- **Hands:** chopsticks or fork lifting food → medium close-up, slow, simple motion.
- **No text** on menus, signs or packaging — add in editing.

## Look Card (default)

`Look: warm appetizing color, soft side light, shallow depth of field, 50–85mm lens feel, real textures.`

## Templates

### 1. Signature dish close-up 招牌菜特写
```
{Dish with key ingredients, colors and textures} in a {bowl / plate material} on a {table surface}.
0–4s: Steam rises slowly from the dish; {sauce glistens / oil shimmers}.
4–7s: {Chopsticks lift one piece slowly}; the final frame holds.
Camera: close-up at a 30-degree angle, slow push-in, 85mm lens feel, shallow depth of field.
Light: warm side light from the left, a soft highlight on the {sauce}.
Style: realistic food commercial, appetizing warm palette.
Sound: gentle sizzling, soft restaurant ambience.
No text, no logos.
```

### 2. Cooking process 制作过程
```
A chef's hands {action: tossing noodles in a wok / slicing fish / plating} in a busy open kitchen.
0–3s: {first step}.
3–6s: {second step}; flames or steam rise.
6–8s: The finished dish is placed on the pass; the frame holds.
Camera: medium close-up, gimbal-smooth follow, 35mm lens feel.
Light: warm kitchen light with a bright highlight from the flame.
Sound: sizzling, clanging wok, kitchen bustle.
No text; hands move naturally, not too fast.
```

### 3. Atmosphere 店内氛围
```
{Restaurant interior: style, seating, lighting fixtures} in the {evening}, {6–10 diners of mixed ages and backgrounds} softly out of focus.
Small motion: candles flicker, a server walks past in the background, glasses catch the light.
Camera: one slow lateral move from the entrance to the bar, eye level, 35mm lens feel.
Light: warm pendant lights, soft practical lamps.
Style: realistic hospitality film, warm muted palette.
Sound: low chatter, soft jazz, cutlery clinks.
No text on signs or menus.
```

# Scene pack — Talking-head & knowledge 口播知识

Use with Mode C (script) and Mode D (B-roll plan). Mode A/B for the background and B-roll shots.

## Pack questions

- 形式？ A. 真人/数字人出镜口播 ｜ B. 纯配音 + 画面（不露脸） ｜ C. 两者混合
- 时长？ A. 15 秒 ｜ B. 30 秒 ｜ C. 60 秒
- 语气？ A. 专业可信 ｜ B. 轻松聊天 ｜ C. 犀利观点

## Pack checks

- **Facts:** no invented statistics, studies or guarantees; if a number is needed, ask the user for the source or mark it `【请核实】`.
- **Structure:** hook (3 s) → 1–3 points → call to action.
- **Visual change every 3–5 seconds** (cut to B-roll, zoom, caption) to hold attention.
- **Lip sync:** presenter shots locked-off, head mostly still (see Mode C).

## Templates

### 1. Presenter background 出镜背景图 (Mode B)
```
A tidy {home office / studio / bookshelf corner} behind a presenter's seat, eye level, medium shot framing with the seat centered and space for the presenter's head and shoulders.
Light: soft key light from the front-left, warm practical lamp in the background, background slightly darker than the subject area.
Style: realistic, warm muted palette, shallow depth of field on the background.
No text, no logos, no screens with readable text.
```

### 2. B-roll shot 配图镜头 (Mode A)
```
{Concrete visual for the point: hands writing a checklist / a city street at dawn / a coffee cup on a desk}.
Small motion: {one motion}.
Camera: {one move}, {lens feel}.
Light: {source}.
Style: realistic documentary.
Sound: no music, natural ambience only.
No readable text.
```

### 3. Script skeleton 口播结构 (Mode C)
```
钩子（3 秒）：{一个反常识问题 / 痛点}
观点 1：{一句话} + {一个例子}
观点 2：{一句话} + {一个例子}
（可选）观点 3
行动号召：{关注 / 评论 / 收藏}
```

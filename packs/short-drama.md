# Scene pack — Short drama 短剧

## Pack questions

- 类型？ A. 逆袭爽剧 ｜ B. 情感/甜宠 ｜ C. 悬疑反转
- 这次写多少？ A. 单个镜头 ｜ B. 一场戏（3–6 个镜头） ｜ C. 先出分镜清单（Mode D）
- 有角色参考图吗？ A. 有 ｜ B. 没有，先出角色设定图

## Pack checks

- **Character Cards first:** every recurring character gets a card (age, hair, clothing, one distinctive detail) used word for word in every shot.
- **≤ 2 characters interacting per shot;** extras stay in the soft background.
- **One emotion beat per shot**, at least 2–3 seconds to land.
- **Dialogue timing:** ≤ 2.5 English words or ≤ 4 Chinese characters per second; one speaker per line; only on platforms that generate speech — otherwise mark the line for voiceover.
- **Hook** in the first 3 seconds of episode 1; each episode ends on an open question (cliffhanger).
- **Continuity:** next shot starts from the last frame of the previous shot when the action continues.
- Original characters and stories only; no copyrighted characters or real people.

## Scene structure

```
场次：第 1 集 · 第 2 场 ｜ 地点：… ｜ 时间：… ｜ 情绪曲线：平静 → 震惊 → 冷笑
Look Card：…
Character Cards：…
Shot 1 (3s) … / Shot 2 (4s) … / Shot 3 (3s) …
```

## Templates

### 1. Reveal shot 反转揭示
```
{Character Card A} stands in {setting}, {lighting}.
0–3s: She holds a {document / phone} and reads it, her face calm.
3–6s: Her eyes widen slightly, her grip tightens, she looks up slowly toward the camera.
Camera: locked-off medium close-up, eye level, 50mm lens feel, shallow depth of field.
Light: cool side light from a window, the other half of her face in soft shadow.
Style: realistic drama, muted cool palette.
Sound: low tense drone, a single paper rustle.
No text on the document or screen.
```

### 2. Confrontation 对峙
```
{Character Card A} and {Character Card B} face each other across {a desk / a doorway} in {setting}.
0–3s: B says, coldly: "{≤ 7 words}"
3–6s: A pauses, then a small smile appears.
6–9s: A turns and walks out of frame; B stays still; the frame holds.
Camera: locked-off over-the-shoulder shot from behind B, eye level, 35mm lens feel.
Light: hard top light, deep shadows.
Sound: room tone, footsteps, no music until the end.
No text, no logos.
```

### 3. Establishing shot 场景交代
```
{Location: office tower lobby / old courtyard / rainy street} at {time}, {weather}.
Small motion: {rain on the glass, people pass in the far background}.
Camera: one slow crane down from {high point} to {entrance}, 24mm lens feel.
Light: {cool blue hour with warm windows}.
Style: realistic drama, film look.
Sound: city ambience, light rain.
No text on signs.
```

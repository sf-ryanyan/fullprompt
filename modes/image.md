# Mode B — Image

## Structure

```
[Subject with concrete details]
[Setting and time of day]
Composition: [shot size, angle, subject position, foreground/background, empty space for titles]
Camera: [lens feel, depth of field]
Light: [named source]
Style: [one look; color palette]
[Guardrail line]
```

## Composition checklist

- Shot size: wide / medium / close-up.
- Angle: eye level / low angle / high angle / top-down (flat lay).
- Subject position: centered, or on the left/right third.
- Empty space: "leave the top third empty for a title" when text will be added later.
- Foreground interest: "out-of-focus leaves in the foreground".

## Ratio guide

| Use | Ratio |
|---|---|
| 小红书 / Instagram post | 3:4 or 4:5 |
| 抖音 / Reels / Stories / video first frame | 9:16 |
| YouTube cover, website banner, listing photo | 16:9 |
| Square post, product on white | 1:1 |
| Photo print look | 3:2 |

## Video first frame

If the image will be animated later:
- Describe the exact first moment of the clip (pose, position).
- Use the same Look Card and Character Card as the video.
- Use the video's ratio.
- Leave room for the planned motion (e.g. space in front of a walking person).

## Text in the image

- Default: no text — add titles in editing.
- If the user wants text and the tool renders text well (see image-platforms.md): exact words in double quotes, position, size, font feel, 1–6 words, then "no other text".

## Product photos

- Keep the real product accurate: for a real product, recommend editing/compositing from the user's own product photo rather than inventing it.
- Describe surface (matte, glossy), material, and a clean background or a simple real setting.

## Example

Input: "咖啡店海报背景图，要有高级感"

```
A ceramic pour-over coffee set on a walnut counter in a small, quiet café, early morning.
Composition: medium shot at eye level, the coffee set on the right third, the top 40% of the frame left as a softly blurred cream wall for a title.
Camera: 50mm lens feel, shallow depth of field.
Light: soft side window light from the left, a thin wisp of steam catching the light.
Style: realistic editorial photography, warm muted palette of cream, walnut and charcoal, light film grain.
No text, no logos.
```
